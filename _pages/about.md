I am an M.S. student in Computer Science at West Virginia University. My research interests are in trustworthy AI, uncertainty quantification, and understanding when machine learning models become unreliable.

I currently work on conformal methods for detecting changes that affect world-model predictions. I have also developed a representation-aware conformal calibration method for deep survival models, with a paper under review at IEEE JBHI. In another project, I studied catastrophic forgetting and cross-modal interference in vision-language models; that work is in preparation for submission to CVPR 2027.

I also work with the WVU Eye Institute on a retinal imaging project for diabetic retinopathy severity prediction and collaborate with the Rockefeller Neuroscience Institute on 3D analysis of neural connections.

I am interested in pursuing a Ph.D. beginning in Fall 2027.
